## What's in each folder

- **`heimdall-data-dossier.md`**: the output of the Heimdall data-analysis agent, run before the article is drafted:
  - **STAT_ROW**: verified quantitative data points with sources and dates
  - **RISK_TABLE**: technical risk assessments with severity ratings and barrier descriptions
  - **TREND_PROJECTIONS**: near-, mid- and long-term forecasts with confidence levels and citations
  - Topic-specific sections (patent landscape, market data, regulatory timelines, etc.)
- **`review-copy.md`**: the full draft as submitted for human review at the publish gate. Edits made during review are applied to the published article.

[`mimir-dossiers/`](mimir-dossiers/) holds the Mimir research agent's source dossier (`01_mimir_dossier.md`) for each topic, which is the starting material for everything above.

Sources are drawn from arXiv, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Google Patents, USPTO, and IETF.

## Why we publish this

Developers share useful repositories faster than they share articles. If you find the raw data useful (for your own research, to verify our claims, or to build on), fork it. If you spot errors, open an issue.
